I am a Doctoral Researcher in Political Science at the Department of Political Science at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, where I work at the chair for Political Theory and Public Policy (Prof. Dr. Claudia Landwehr).

My research examines how citizens and political elites understand democracy and which democratic institutions and decision-making procedures they prefer. I work with survey data and survey experiments, including factorial survey designs, and with comparative surveys of legislators in Germany and the United States. A further strand of my work looks at how political socialization in authoritarian contexts shapes citizens' attitudes towards democracy.

I currently work in the DFG-funded project *False Consensus Beliefs in Politics and their Effects on Legitimacy* (Research Group Big Structural Change). Until August 2026, I worked in the DFG-funded project *Conceptions of Democracy among Political Elites and Citizens*.

You can find my publications on the [Publications](/publications/) page. My full academic CV is available on the [CV](https://leastallbaum.github.io/cv/) page.
